See the web

See how to get started ↓

Made for Safari

Your pages, in Aurebesh.

Holonet is a Safari Web Extension inside a lightweight app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It changes how page text looks using fonts bundled with the app; it does not rewrite the page’s words.

A quick setup

Enable Holonet in Safari.

iPhone and iPad

  1. 01

    Open Safari settings

    In Settings, choose Safari. On newer versions, find Safari under Apps.

  2. 02

    Turn on the extension

    Choose Extensions, select Holonet, and turn on Allow Extension.

  3. 03

    Choose website access

    Allow access on the websites where you want Holonet to change the font.

  4. 04

    Pick a reading mode

    In Safari, open the Page menu, choose Holonet, and select a font mode.

Mac

  1. 01

    Open Safari settings

    In Safari, choose Safari > Settings from the menu bar.

  2. 02

    Turn on the extension

    Select Extensions, choose Holonet, and enable it.

  3. 03

    Choose website access

    Use the Holonet toolbar button to allow access to the websites you choose.

  4. 04

    Pick a reading mode

    Select the toolbar button, turn Holonet on, and choose a font mode.

Two ways to read

Choose your script style.

A

Standard

Shows each Latin letter with its matching Aurebesh character.

CH

Digraphs

Uses combined characters for pairs such as ch, sh, and th.

Private by design

Your browsing stays yours.

Holonet stores its two settings on your device. It does not collect or send your browsing history or webpage content.
