Made for Safari

Your pages, in Aurebesh.

Holonet is a Safari Web Extension inside a lightweight iPhone and iPad app. It changes how page text looks using fonts bundled with the app; it does not rewrite the page’s words.

A quick setup

Enable Holonet in Safari.

  1. 01

    Open Safari settings

    In Settings, choose Safari. On newer iOS versions, find Safari under Apps.

  2. 02

    Turn on the extension

    Choose Extensions, select Holonet, and turn on Allow Extension.

  3. 03

    Choose website access

    Allow access on the websites where you want Holonet to change the font. Safari lets you choose or change access at any time.

  4. 04

    Pick a reading mode

    In Safari, open the Page menu and choose Holonet. Turn it on and choose a font mode.

Two ways to read

Choose your script style.

A

Standard

Shows each Latin letter with its matching Aurebesh character.

CH

Digraphs

Uses combined characters for pairs such as ch, sh, and th.

Private by design

Your browsing stays yours.

Holonet stores its two settings on your device. It does not collect or send your browsing history or webpage content.
