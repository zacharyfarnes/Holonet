Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

Holonet is a Safari extension that changes the appearance of webpage text using fonts bundled with the app. This policy describes the Holonet app and extension.

Information Holonet handles

Holonet does not collect or transmit personal information, browsing history, or webpage content. It has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting service, or app backend.

The extension saves only two preferences on your device: whether Holonet is enabled and whether Digraph mode is selected. These preferences use Safari’s local extension storage and are not sent to Holonet or another service.

Webpage access

Safari asks you to control which websites an extension can access. Holonet needs that permission to apply its bundled typeface to a page. The extension changes the page’s presentation in your browser; it does not send the page or its contents anywhere. You can review or change Holonet’s website access in Safari settings.

Children’s privacy

Holonet does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Holonet’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the changed version is released. The effective date above will also change.
